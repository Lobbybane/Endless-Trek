# Endless Trek

A hiker and his white husky, Ghost, walking an endless mountain trail. It's a single-file three.js (r170) scene.

**Play it:** https://claude.ai/artifact/BVmtRPfnyDkFBa29kfKerH

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. It loads three.js from unpkg, so it needs a network connection. Everything else is generated in code: the models, textures, weather and sound.

## Controls

| Button | Key | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day / Night | `D` | Jumps to nightfall or sunrise over 3 seconds. The clock also runs on its own. |
| Weather | `W` | Cycles Clear Skies → Partly Cloudy → Full Clouds → Dark Rain Clouds → Raining → Snowing → Heavy Snow → Violent Storm |
| Break | `B` | "Stay, Ghost." A 5–7 second rest: catching his breath, a snack (Ghost gets a treat) or a pet |
| Customize | `C` | Opens the Outfitter: 3 jackets, 3 pants, 3 pairs of shoes and 3 beanies. The choice is remembered. |
| Sound | `M` | Turns the procedural audio on or off |

Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom. When idle, the camera slowly sways around the pair. Click the title card to collapse it.

## On the trail

- **The trail:** a dense forest of spruce, pine, fir and birch, with boulders, ferns, flowers, mushrooms, fallen logs, cairns and trail markers. There are signposts to Raccoon City (the distance counts down), Bear Country warnings, creeks with footbridges, and lanterns that come on after dark.
- **Overlooks:** every so often the trees open up at a fenced overlook. Out in the valley you can see Eagle Falls (with a rainbow on sunny days), Raccoon City (its lights come on at night), Silver Lake or Lookout Peak. The hiker stops to take a photo.
- **The hiker:** makes phone calls, hunts for a signal, pets Ghost, leans on a pine for a drink, scratches, ties his laces, adjusts his straps, plays fetch, reads signs, and waves to passing hikers.
- **Ghost:** stays close, but trots ahead or lags behind to sniff bushes, mark trees, drink from creeks, greet passers-by (and their dogs), bark at bears, bound through snow and shake off rain.
- **Wildlife:** black bears, deer, red foxes, hares, squirrels, songbirds, geese, eagles over the overlooks, an owl at camp, and fireflies at night.
- **Night:** he pitches the tent, builds a fire ring, drags a log over to sit on, cooks dinner while Ghost eats from his bowl, pets him by the fire and turns in. In the morning he douses the fire and packs up. The fire ring and log stay behind on the trail.
- **Storms:** in Heavy Snow or a Violent Storm he pitches the tent straight away, makes only the fire, and gets Ghost inside with him until it passes.
- **Weather details:** snow settles on trees and the ground, rain soaks and darkens everything, footprints form in snow and mud, his breath shows in the cold, and lightning flashes across the clouds.
